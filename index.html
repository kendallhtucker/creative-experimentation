BRIAN BOX STUNT

Year: 2025 Location: Flatiron Plaza, NYC Runtime: 8 hours, live
214M
views
1
box
600K
receipts

ramp processes 600,000 receipts a day. so we put brian baumgartner in a glass box in flatiron plaza and made him try to process every single one of them by hand. in public. for 8 hours straight.

the premise: ramp hired brian as our spokesperson. brian thinks he was hired as our new chief accounting officer. he's been training for the role for 20 years (on tv). nobody had the heart to tell him about the software.

every hour the internet voted on what happened to him next. two buttons: help him, or make it worse. if "make it worse" won, the box opened and a fresh avalanche of receipts rained down on him. people voted to make it worse. a lot.

we livestreamed the whole thing across five platforms. about 2,100 people came to flatiron in person to watch a man in a box do paperwork. millions more watched online.

📰
Marketing Brew "Inside the making of Ramp's viral livestream with Brian Baumgartner"

at the end of the day, eric walked into the box and "rescued" brian with the software. brian's verdict: "accounting has changed since my day."

the moment got loud enough that we named october 15 national expense report day. it stuck.

after brian in a box blew up, we knew our super bowl moment had to be brian again. the question was how to do a super bowl ad in a way people would actually talk about.

the image we kept coming back to was ten brians showing up at the game together. what is better than one brian? many brians. ramp multiplies what's possible. we worked backwards from that stunt idea into our ad.

"Multiply What's Possible" — Ramp Super Bowl LX commercial

a super bowl ad on its own is the easy part. the harder part is making the moment feel inevitable everywhere the camera isn't. so we built the rest of it too.

we rolled out 10 pieces of original social content across launch week.

@ramp.com on tiktok

we threw a brian-themed party in san francisco the day of the game, where guests came dressed as brian.

at the party, we ran a brian baumgartner look-alike contest.

the two winners got tickets to the super bowl.

we shipped bald caps nationwide to anyone who wanted to help multiply brian.

and we actually brought 10 brians to the game!!
